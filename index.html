Fretboard interval visualiser

One example of every interval, measured from the root. Tap any note to move the root. Toggle a group to hide its dots — the written explanations stay below, so students can work out where the notes are. Tap a name in the list to reveal or check its position on the board.

The root and the perfect intervals (4th, 5th, octave) are the fixed framework. Major intervals (2nd, 3rd, 6th, 7th) are the bright tones of a major key; minor intervals (♭3rd, ♭6th, ♭7th) are their flattened counterparts, in key for a minor key. Hide a group and the descriptions below still tell you where each note sits — a good way to test recall.
